What is an Oopbuy spreadsheet?

An Oopbuy spreadsheet is a personal table of product references used to organize shopping research. Useful fields include the listing URL, seller, selected variant, measurement notes and the date the listing was last checked.

What is an Oopbuy spreadsheet?

An Oopbuy spreadsheet is a personal table of product references used to organize shopping research. Useful fields include the listing URL, seller, selected variant, measurement notes and the date the listing was last checked.

Is this an official Oopbuy website?

No. This is an independent research guide. Its browsing links lead to CAFANS, and it does not operate an Oopbuy checkout or account service.

Can I import these category links directly into Oopbuy?

CAFANS category URLs are browsing destinations, not guaranteed importable marketplace product links. Open a listing, identify its original product URL where available and check whether your chosen service supports that URL.

How do I keep a product shortlist useful over time?

Record the exact variant and review date, mark unavailable listings, and recheck the destination before ordering. Keep an older URL in your notes when replacing it so you can identify what changed.

Do the brand photographs confirm CAFANS stock or authenticity?

No. The photographs are visual references. They do not establish availability, seller authorization, authenticity or a relationship between the brands and CAFANS.
